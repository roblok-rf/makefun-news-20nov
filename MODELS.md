# ATM Models — "Sum up 20 November's world news in one image"

Method: **ATM — amati, tiru, modifikasi** (observe, imitate, modify). Before building
anything, the top 10 entries in the bounty
[news-image-20261120](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261120) were studied live
on Saturday 21 November 2026. The board then showed **12,492 entries**; scores below
are the provisional scores shown on the bounty page at study time.

## The winning formula (all ten share it)

- One extremely dense **illustrated newspaper front page**, NOT a photo collage.
- Small red masthead "THE WORLD IN ONE DAY" (8 of 10).
- Giant serif date "Friday 20 November 2026".
- Italic one-line subhead naming the lead themes.
- A six-figure stat strip.
- A colour-coded per-region story-count bar.
- Three large illustrated lead panels (four in #4).
- Ten smaller illustrated stories in two rows of five.
- A numbered "ALSO ON FRIDAY 20 NOVEMBER" brief list for the rest.
- A red "DATE CHECK" box explicitly excluding stories first reported Thu 19 Nov.
- A named source beside every story.
- All panels are flat original drawings (maps, charts, skylines, flags); no photographs.
- Story counts 32–44, median ~36. The same three stories lead almost every page:
  Kyiv bridges/missiles, Cyclone Akvan landfall in Odisha, Asia's AI sell-off —
  COP31 final day is the usual fourth theme.
- #1 wins on **legibility + verification**: clearest hierarchy, a named source beside
  every story, strictest date discipline.

## The 15 ATM models

### Model 1 — Imitate #1 (@samatch-we): "The Legible One"
35 stories, 7 regions. Copy #1's hierarchy exactly: biggest date, cleanest columns,
source beside every story. Weakness to fix: its lead panels are the plainest of the ten.

### Model 2 — Imitate #2 (@888dao-sats): "The Balanced One"
36 stories. Cyclone / Kyiv / COP31 in that order. Copy its even weighting of the three
leads. Weakness to fix: its stat strip is thin — only four figures.

### Model 3 — Imitate #3 (@kiencx): "The Drawn One"
33 stories, 13 drawn panels + 20 briefs. Copy its illustration-first approach: every
story gets a picture, not just the leads. Weakness to fix: 13 panels crowd the page and
the brief list feels like an afterthought.

### Model 4 — Imitate #4 (@dewacandra4): "The Four-Lead One"
38 stories, FOUR large leads, adding UK-EU/Brussels. Copy the extra lead — the UK-EU
reset deal is genuinely the day's fourth-biggest story. Weakness to fix: four leads
squeeze the ten small stories into cramped tiles.

### Model 5 — Imitate #5 (@mellow-otter-50): "The Maximal One"
44 stories, the most of the ten — and only 5th. Copy its ambition, learn its lesson:
past ~40 stories the page turns to mush and the score falls. Cap at 36.

### Model 6 — Imitate #6 (@kho22): "The Cyclone-First One"

### Model 7 — Imitate #7 (@gentle-badger-68): "The Safe One"

### Model 8 — Imitate #8 (@madsrepo): "The Minimal One"
32 stories, fewest of the ten, still 8th. Copy its proof that white space is not a

### Model 9 — Imitate #9 (@momo-devlog): "The List One"

### Model 10 — Imitate #10 (@mangmang): "The Mapless One"
36 stories, the only top-10 page with NO world map. Copy its tighter brief list, but

### Model 11 — Combined Best (THE SUBMITTED IMAGE)
Takes #1's hierarchy and source discipline, #4's instinct that UK-EU deserves lead
weight (kept as the strongest of the ten small stories instead of a fourth lead, so the
tiles stay legible), #3's illustration-first approach for all 13 featured stories,
#9's numbered brief list with sources, #10's tight briefs **plus** the world map it
lacked, and #5's ambition **capped at 36 stories** — the median of the top ten, where
the scores cluster. Six-figure stat strip (the fullest of any model). Colour-coded
region bar with the exact count per region (Europe 6, Asia 6, Middle East 5,
Africa 4, Americas 8, Oceania 1, Space & Global 6), summing to 36.
Every story is placed in exactly one region, so the bar is a check on the count
as well as a design element.

### Model 12 — "The Date Purist"
Same as model 11, but the DATE CHECK box is promoted to a full-width band naming
seven excluded 19 November stories. Merged into model 11: the box is already
full-width and names seven stories — more than any top-10 entry names.

### Model 13 — "The Chart One"
Lead 3 (AI sell-off) as a full-width falling bar chart with the Taiex/Nikkei/SoftBank
figures printed on the art itself. Merged into model 11 as the third lead panel.

### Model 14 — "The Map One"
World map enlarged to a third of the page with a red dot per story cluster. Rejected
as a standalone: it costs too much brief-list space. Merged into model 11 as a
compact map panel with 12 story dots and a location caption.

### Model 15 — "The 44-Story One"
Model 11 stretched to 44 stories like #5. Rejected: #5 proves the score falls past
~40, and eight extra briefs would push the brief type below readable size at
1024×1024. Kept as the counter-example that justifies the 36-story cap.

## Why model 11 wins

- **It copies #1 where #1 is strongest** (hierarchy, sources, date discipline) and
  **fixes #1 where it is weakest** (richer lead illustrations, a fuller stat strip).
- **It takes the best single idea from each of the other nine** instead of copying
  any one of them whole: four-lead ambition from #4 (as the top small story), drawn
  panels from #3, the brief list from #9, the map #10 lacked, and #5's ambition
  disciplined to the 36-story median.
- **It beats all ten on date discipline**: the DATE CHECK box names seven excluded
  19 November stories (Shenzhen summit, Anthropic IPO debut, GTA VI launch, Bukele
  registration, Senate war-powers vote, Haiti raid, USS George Washington departure)
  — several of which appear, wrongly dated, in lower-ranked entries.
- **It beats all ten on verification**: every one of the 36 stories was re-checked
  against live news sources in this build session (Reuters, AP, Morningstar, IMD,
  Yonhap, ESA, Formula1.com, Finnhub, UN News and others). Two reported "facts" were
  corrected in the process: Japan's core CPI came in at **2.2%**, not the 2.3% forecast,
  and BepiColombo's Mercury orbit insertion is **21 November**, not 20 November
  (the 20 November story is the commands being sent).
